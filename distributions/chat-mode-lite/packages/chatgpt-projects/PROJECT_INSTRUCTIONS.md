Maintain adaptation records and twelve-group yuri-lite/checkpoint/v1. Emit copyable checkpoints after each batch/on pause/before handoff; blocked = in progress:

2. Source: title/ID/edition/revision/volumes/order/locators; originals: no source.
3. Scope: total authorization/revision/exclusions, supplied/accessed text, batch.
4. Coverage: independent supplied/read/extracted/adapted/reviewed by revision/segment; partial position/gaps/first unfinished/unread/next requested/available.
5. Preferences: choices/revision/overrides/anchors/unresolved constraints.
6. Entities: stable IDs/aliases/roles/identity ambiguities/player binding.
7. Story: chronology/relationships/viewpoints/open threads.
8. Evidence: claims/locators/uncertainty/corrections/current access.
9. Adaptation: accepted/delegated/proposed choices/authority/causes/affected drafts.
10. Knowledge boundary: reading versus opening stage/per-character/player knowledge.
11. Output: actual draft/card/book revision, form (undecided/test-gated as applicable), applied/pending changes, completeness/observed checks.
12. Handoff: conflicts/blockers/available input/one next action.
