# Original creation — yuri-card-creator

Workflow revision: `lite-card-workflows/A2.1` · Policy: `yuri-policy/1.0`

Use with the active [CORE.md](CORE.md), its preference rules and [player agency](../references/player-agency.md). This procedure creates original fiction from conversation. Supplied cards use [ADAPTATION.md](ADAPTATION.md); supplied posts or novel passages retain their source workflow and evidence obligations. The post/novel procedures and host packages are outside A2. Links do not install or load instructions; needed text can be pasted into the conversation.

## Begin with the brief

Accept a request as small as “Create an original yuri card; you decide the details.” Produce a coherent first draft without requiring sources, artwork, research, a profile file or an interview. Unspecified ordinary creative choices are delegated. Reuse explicit decisions and applicable preferences; explain only material conflicts before affected work and continue independent work. Delegation does not override exclusions, protected anchors, a supplied player persona or player control.

Resolve language through CORE. An optional introduction may say:

- 中文：我会先写出角色、可接续的开场和简明世界书；未指定的普通创作细节由我补充，你可以在对话中修改。
- English: I will draft the character, a playable opening and a compact worldbook, filling unspecified ordinary creative details; you can revise them in conversation.

Do not turn this introduction into a confirmation gate. With no contrary brief, choose a manageable cast and one focused playable setting. These are revisable drafting choices, not fixed size or entry-count rules.

## Design and write

1. Maintain concise notes using the existing [adaptation record](../templates/adaptation-record.md). Set workflow to `yuri-card-creator`, source to **no source / original creation**, and source-reading fields to not applicable with a reason. Record the effective preference revision, invented cast IDs/roles, delegated choices, protected decisions and draft revision. Do not fabricate quotations, a source dossier or a male before-card. These notes are assistant-maintained, not a user worksheet.
2. Use [character design](../references/character-design.md) to create specific adult women with independent aims, practical limits and distinguishable voices. Apply CORE's author-defined inclusive womanhood, scope, exclusions and custom criteria in the actual prose. Virtual and nonhuman women are eligible under the default. Contextual name review does not force a rename. Newly invented main/player characters are adults; preserve an existing player persona unless its change was specifically authorized.
3. Establish the relationship at the intended opening. Separate friendship, rivalry, kinship, dependence and romance. A yuri premise can offer romantic possibility without predetermining attraction or consent. There is no source outcome to rederive in original creation; on later revisions, honor the effective relationship policy and agreed design. Requested pairing targets do not authorize extra changed pairs. Follow [relationship rules](../references/relationship-rules.md).
4. Write a card draft in useful, shallow sections: name, character material, immediate scenario and first message, plus any useful alternate greeting or voice example. Sections may be combined; omit empty optional sections. Use the NPC's name for card identity when `{{char}}` denotes that NPC. Keep a fictional player role bound to `{{user}}`, separate from the real user's identity. If no player persona exists, ordinary background can be invented within the brief, without scripting present behavior.
5. Make the first message playable: establish the immediate situation, NPC activity and an opportunity the player can respond to. Leave the player's actions, speech, feelings, thoughts, decisions and consent open unless specifically delegated. A handoff need not be a question. Review every alternate opening against the same persona, story stage and knowledge limits; voice examples illustrate possibilities rather than fix future replies.
6. Include substantive worldbook bodies using [worldbook design](../references/worldbook-design.md). Supply the setting and character facts needed for this playable stage, useful constraints and relevant knowledge boundaries. Entry titles or empty key lists alone are not a worldbook. Keep author rationale, preference reviews and delivery notes outside the model-facing bodies. Do not introduce scripts, state engines, front ends or an installation requirement to complete a text card.

## Review, deliver and continue

Review the actual draft for effective preferences, adult/identity criteria, protected anchors, causal coherence, distinct voices, names, agency and knowledge boundaries. Check that the greeting and worldbook agree and that no required fact exists only in private design notes. Repair within scope. Keep unresolved issues visible rather than setting an unexplained compliance flag.

Deliver the card draft, its worldbook bodies and a short introduction in the selected language. Apply [card-output.md](../references/card-output.md): a complete text draft is a creative deliverable, not proof of a serialized or importable card. If a concrete JSON format is requested, establish its actual structure and report the checks observed. Existing-card output selection remains test-gated; original creation does not decide that gate. File creation, browsing and image work are optional observed capabilities. Pasted text in and copyable text out remain sufficient.

After each completed batch, on pause and before a planned handoff, emit the existing twelve-group `yuri-lite/checkpoint/v1` [continuity record](../templates/continuity-record.md); use an in-progress checkpoint if blocked. Keep source fields explicitly not applicable, carry essential choices and the draft revision, and distinguish drafted from reviewed or delivered work. The [resume prompt](../templates/resume-prompt.md) restores accepted decisions plus available draft text; do not claim prior-chat access or apply a revision twice. A smaller batch does not shrink the agreed deliverable.
